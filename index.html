WeBox 3.0 Discontinued

Sorry but...

I honestly never wanted to write this message, but we have officially hit the end of the line. WeBox is officially discontinued.

Building this platform was a massive passion project for me. I put so many late nights into crafting every single detail—from setting up the arcade games, videos, music feeds, and soundboards, to trying to keep the live Azerbaijan TV streams running and building real-time messages and Discord-style servers. Seeing everything come together piece by piece was an incredible feeling, and having you all test it, use it, and post on it meant the absolute world to me.

It breaks my heart to say this, but managing all the backend bugs, API key issues, database errors, storage limits, and endless sync glitches on my own became completely overwhelming. No matter how many times I tried to patch it or rewrite the code to keep it afloat, things kept breaking under the surface. It got to a point where maintaining WeBox in its current form was causing more frustration than joy, and it just wasn't fair to leave you guys with a half-broken experience.

"It hurts so much to shut down something you built from scratch with pure heart and hard work, but sometimes you have to close one chapter to build something bigger and better."

Please don't be too sad though—this might not be the complete end forever. I am taking a step back to breathe and rethink everything. There is a real chance that I will bring WeBox back in the future with a massive, ground-up rebuild (WeBox 4.0). If and when that happens, it will be cleaner, faster, completely bug-free, and built on a rock-solid system that won't fall apart.

Thank you from the bottom of my heart to everyone who supported WeBox, posted content, played games, and stayed with me through every update. Your support kept me going.

Ulvi Zakirov Creator & Developer of WeBox
