Service Offline

Sorry but...

WeBox is currently down for maintenance and offline indefinitely while we sort out some server issues and rethink how everything works behind the scenes. Between managing the storage buckets, sync loops, and keeping up with the database stuff, things were getting pretty messy to maintain smoothly in its current form.

We didn't want to just keep pushing out half-broken patches, so we're taking the whole thing offline for now. But don't worry—this isn't necessarily the end of WeBox. We're actually considering rebuilding it from the ground up with a massive new update. If we bring it back, expect a much cleaner UI, stable video and music hosting, smooth live TV streams, direct chats that don't randomly drop, and proper arcade game support that actually works reliably without weird key glitches or database errors.

Note: Keep your local code and files safe! If we launch WeBox 4.0, you'll be able to link right back in.
